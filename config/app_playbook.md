# The Remake Playbook

Feeds §A of the brief. The question it answers: **what do I build that makes
money, without inventing anything new?**

## The one fact that should govern every decision

Building is no longer the hard part. He can ship in a weekend what used to
take a funded team a quarter. **Distribution is the hard part**, and it is
the part AI did not make easier — it arguably made it worse, because
everyone else can ship fast now too.

So the rule: **do not build a standalone consumer app.** Nobody will find
it. Build where buyers are already shopping with a card out.

Marketplaces with built-in distribution and paying users:

| Store | Who buys | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Shopify App Store** | merchants losing money daily | strongest — they pay to fix revenue leaks |
| **Chrome Web Store** | everyone | cheapest to build, huge traffic |
| **Figma / Notion / Slack / WordPress** | teams with budgets | warm, software-literate buyers |
| **Framer / Webflow** | designers | taste-led, which is his edge |

The proof: the solo-founder successes are overwhelmingly ecosystem tools.
EZ Fulfill ($8K MRR, 1,200 Shopify stores, built on weekends). PODTurbo
($25K MRR). Bannerbear (~$1M/yr). Testimonial.to ($1M+/yr). Not one of them
is a new idea. All of them sit next to someone else's checkout.

## The four remakes — pick ONE, never invent

**1. Same tool, one audience.** Take a general product and rebuild it for a
narrow group whose needs it ignores. Generic invoicing → invoicing for
Indian freelancers with GST and UPI built in. Narrow wins because you can
write copy that makes one group feel seen, and because incumbents will not
chase a segment that small.

**2. Same tool, better taste.** Most B2B software is functional and ugly.
Design is a real moat because competitors cannot copy judgment, only
features. **This is his strongest edge** — he studies how things are made
and has a filmmaker's eye. It is the rare case where caring about beauty is
a commercial advantage rather than a distraction.

**3. Same tool, unbundled.** Find the bloated product where everyone uses
one feature. Ship only that feature, cheaper and faster. Bloat is an
incumbent's weakness disguised as a moat.

**4. Same tool, different price shape.** Flip subscription to one-time, or
per-seat to flat. People resent a 12th subscription more than they resent
the price.

## Where demand is already proven

Do not brainstorm. Demand leaves evidence:

- **1- and 2-star reviews on highly-installed apps.** High installs = real
  market. Low rating = unmet need. That gap is the whole opportunity, and
  users describe it for you in their own words.
- **"alternative to X" searches.** People actively shopping for a
  replacement, wallet already open.
- **"Is there a tool that…" threads** in the relevant subreddit.
- **Acquisition listings** on Acquire.com / Flippa — real revenue on real
  products, which tells you what a category actually earns before you build.
- **Support forums of a big platform.** Merchants describing what breaks.

## Honest numbers, so the target is real

- Micro SaaS under $10K MRR sells at **2–3.5x ARR**.
- $8K MRR at 65% margin ≈ $62K/yr profit ≈ **~$250K at a 4x multiple**.
- Solo micro SaaS typically lands **$1K–$30K MRR**. That is the realistic
  band, and $2K MRR is life-changing for a student in India.
- **Owner dependency sets the multiple.** A product needing 40 hrs/week
  sells at ~2x; the same product needing 5 hrs/week sells at 4–5x. Build to
  be boring and automatic, not impressive and manual.

## Filters — kill an idea fast if

- It needs network effects to be useful on day one (chicken-and-egg).
- The buyer and the user are different people and neither has budget.
- It is consumer, free, and ad-supported. That is a venture lottery ticket.
- It needs his constant attention to deliver value. See multiples above.
- He cannot name, today, three specific people who would pay for it.

## What §A must produce every issue

Not inspiration. **One candidate with evidence**, in this shape:

```
THE INCUMBENT     the tool being remade, with installs/revenue if known
THE COMPLAINT     a real quote from a real review or thread, linked
WHICH REMAKE      one of the four above, named
THE BUYER         who pays, and roughly what they pay now
WHY HIM           what he has that the incumbent lacks — usually taste or speed
FIRST STEP        something doable in a day, not a plan
```

No candidate without a linked complaint from a real user. An idea with no
evidence of demand is a daydream with formatting.
